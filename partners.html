Partners and investors.

We are building three communities, and we are looking for the people who can help us build them well. Capital, land, expertise and trade partners all matter here.

Where we need partners.

This is a development business with a social purpose, and the two are not in conflict. Attainable, well designed housing that people actually own is an underserved market, not a charity case.

  • Capital partners

    Investors aligned with a long horizon and a real social return alongside the financial one.

  • Land

    Sites in and around Phoenix, Colorado and Kansas, and landowners open to a conversation about what could go there.

  • Design and construction

    Architects, container fabricators, and general contractors who have done modular work well.

  • Operations and services

    Community management, landscape, home care providers, and hospitality partners.

  • Advisors

    Counsel and advisors experienced in cooperative ownership, fair housing compliance and community formation.

The social impact.

Social isolation is now understood to carry measurable health consequences, and most of what gets built makes it worse: detached houses with no shared ground, or managed buildings with no ownership. Neither produces neighbors.

Well designed, privately owned, genuinely connected housing at an attainable price addresses that directly. Every community we complete is a working argument that it can be done properly and sustainably.
