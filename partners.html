Partners and investors.

We are building three communities, and we are looking for the people who can help us build them well. Capital, land, expertise and trade partners all matter here.

Where we need partners.

This is a development business with a social purpose, and the two are not in conflict. Housing designed around how women actually want to live in later life is an underserved market, not a charity case.

  • Capital partners

    Investors aligned with a long horizon and a real social return alongside the financial one.

  • Land

    Sites in and around Phoenix, Colorado and Kansas, and landowners open to a conversation about what could go there.

  • Design and construction

    Architects, container fabricators, and general contractors who have done modular work well.

  • Operations and services

    Community management, landscape, home care providers, and hospitality partners.

  • Advisors

    Counsel and advisors experienced in cooperative ownership, senior housing and community formation.

The social impact.

Women outlive men, earn less over a lifetime, and are far more likely to face later life alone and with thinner savings. The housing built for them was designed around care delivery, not around living, and the isolation that follows carries real health consequences.

Building well designed, owned, connected housing at an attainable buy in addresses that directly. Every community we complete is a working argument that this can be done properly and sustainably.
